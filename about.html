Beyond the Finish Line
Our Story

Where this started.

NHNE Athletics was founded by believers and former athletes who recognized a crisis in sports culture — athletes chasing external achievement while quietly struggling with burnout, exhaustion, identity pressure, and a lack of purpose.

We exist to restore identity, awaken purpose, and anchor faith in God — using sport as a platform to point everything back to Him.

Our Why Meet the Staff
The Problem

Outside of your sport, do you know who you are?

What happens when you base your value & identity on your performance.

  • Struggles with identity and self-value
  • Overwhelming pressure and anxiety
  • Lack of purpose & direction

"Many athletes are searching for a physical solution to a spiritual problem."

Our Why

Running for the crown that endures.

Sport is the platform. Transformation is the point.

Spiritual Eyes Enlightened

True purpose and identity comes when God opens the eyes of your heart.

Ephesians 1:17–18 Hover to reveal Tap to reveal

17 I keep asking that the God of our Lord Jesus Christ, the glorious Father, may give you the Spirit of wisdom and revelation, so that you may know him better.

18 I pray that the eyes of your heart may be enlightened in order that you may know the hope to which he has called you, the riches of his glorious inheritance in his holy people.

The Crown That Endures

Trophies fade, but purpose remains. Earthly success is temporary — the crown of righteousness lasts forever.

1 Corinthians 9:25 Hover to reveal Tap to reveal

25 Everyone who competes in the games goes into strict training. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get a crown that will last forever.
