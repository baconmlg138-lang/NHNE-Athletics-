Our Story

Where this started.

NHNE Athletics was founded by believers and former athletes who recognized a crisis in sports culture — athletes chasing external achievement while quietly struggling with burnout, exhaustion, identity pressure, and a lack of purpose.

We exist to restore identity, awaken purpose, and anchor faith in God — using sport as a platform to point everything back to Him.

The Problem

Outside of your sport, do you know who you are?

What happens when you base your value & identity on your performance.

  • Struggles with identity and self-value
  • Overwhelming pressure and anxiety
  • Lack of purpose & direction

"Many athletes are searching for a physical solution to a spiritual problem."
